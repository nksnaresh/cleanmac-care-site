FAQ

Is anything ever lost forever?

Not on the first pass. Cleanup moves items to macOS Trash and tracks them in Quarantine while Finder Trash still has them. Finder may empty Trash sooner based on your macOS settings.

How does app uninstall work?

CleanMac Care can remove app bundles and leftovers that macOS allows it to access. Protected files remain visible with a permission explanation instead of being silently ignored.

Where's my data?

On your Mac. We never send file contents, names, or paths off your machine. Anonymous crash reports are opt-in only.

Apple Silicon and Intel?

Universal binary. Both first-class.
