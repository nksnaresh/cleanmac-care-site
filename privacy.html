Privacy Policy

Clean Care is designed to inspect and clean files locally on your Mac. We do not operate an analytics service and do not sell personal information.

Short version: file names, paths, file contents, scan results, and diagnostic logs stay on your Mac unless you explicitly export and share them.

Information the app accesses

Clean Care accesses folders you explicitly select through macOS, and may access your Photos library only after you grant Photos permission. It uses this access to calculate storage usage, identify cleanup candidates, locate duplicates, and perform actions you request.

Information we collect

Clean Care does not include advertising, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, or an account system. The app does not transmit your file names, paths, file contents, scan results, or local diagnostics to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may process purchase history, account, payment, and storefront information under Apple's privacy policy. Clean Care receives only the StoreKit entitlement information needed to determine whether Pro features are active.

Local data and retention

Preferences, security-scoped folder bookmarks, scan summaries, and the Quarantine manifest are stored locally in the app's sandbox. Cleanup sends items to macOS Trash and tracks them only while Trash still contains them. You can revoke folder access or remove the app to clear app-managed local data.

System logs and support

Diagnostic logs are generated and retained locally by macOS. They are shared with us only if you deliberately export and send a diagnostic report. Before sharing, review the report for information you do not want to disclose.

Children and international use

Clean Care is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. Because the app does not operate a user-data service, we do not use personal data for profiling or advertising in any country.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact method on the Support page.
